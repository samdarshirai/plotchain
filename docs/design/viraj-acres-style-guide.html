Guardrails

Do

  • Keep tables and forms on Parchment with Warm Charcoal text — legibility over branding, always.
  • Spend Fraunces on titles and hero figures only.
  • Limit the Seal Card to one per screen.

Don't

  • Use Oxblood for error states — that's --status-danger's job.
  • Let Ink chrome spread into content areas; it kills table contrast.
  • Add scroll reveals or gradients beyond the ones defined here.
